What this teaches

  • Three of five failures never crash. The refund, the total, and the price all end with a polite, wrong answer.
  • Guards are generic; they catch failure shapes. The error guard rescues the "#1004" crash; the repeat guard ends the loop. Neither can see that $1,622.79 is wrong.
  • Confident wrong answers need root-cause fixes — a tool that reports its count, a tool that returns retail price — or an evaluation that checks the numbers (HOS 4).
